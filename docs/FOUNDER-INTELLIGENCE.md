## Definitions
Registered = auth.users. Recent sign-ins = last_sign_in_at within window, not DAU. Activated = username plus active link, not proof of publishing or visits. Complete = name and avatar. Paid status = active known paid plan with Stripe subscription ID in Supabase, not proof of cash receipt. Revenue = active known Stripe subscription gross recurring list prices normalised monthly, separate currencies, excludes discounts, taxes, fees, refunds and trialing subscriptions. Not net or realised revenue. Churn unavailable until historical subscription lifecycle data is collected.
Checkout events use server-created session IDs; paid completion from signed Stripe webhook. Unconfirmed after 24h is not confirmed abandonment. Tracking begins at deployment; legacy payment links bypass these events. Stage 02 PR #124 must be reconciled and merged with this branch to enable the authenticated checkout path for pricing pages. Preserve the founder-event hooks during merge conflicts. No backfill is fabricated.
Browser events are authenticated, daily deduplicated and self-reported; not trusted payment events. No raw email, IP or customer identities exposed in the new aggregate dashboard.
